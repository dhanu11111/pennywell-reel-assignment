# AI prompts used / reproducible prompts

## Trend research prompt
Find three India-focused social trends reported during the latest seven days. For each, provide the trend name, reporting window, source link, reported evidence and a plausible money angle for young working adults aged 22–35. Do not invent view counts. Separate trend evidence from your interpretation.

## Hook and script prompt
Write ten first-three-second hooks connecting a current Diwali outfit trend to practical budgeting for young working adults. Select the strongest three. Write a 30–45 second vertical-video script with timecodes, voice-over, on-screen text, visual direction and one clear CTA. Keep the tone friendly and non-judgmental. Avoid specific financial product recommendations and guaranteed outcomes.

## Storyboard image prompt
Create an original 9:16 editorial illustration for a short personal-finance Reel about festive shopping within a budget. Use a warm cream background, deep navy typography, teal and gold accents, simple geometric shapes, no real people, no brand logos, no legible generated text. Leave a clear text-safe area in the lower third. Maintain the same visual style across eight frames.

## Video assembly prompt
Create an approximately 42-second 9:16 video from eight storyboard frames. Use readable English captions, a calm English voice-over, subtle low-volume original instrumental background, clean cuts, and no copyrighted music. Keep the CTA visible at the end.

## Quality-check prompt
Check that the video is 9:16, 30–45 seconds, captions are readable, narration matches the script, the trend evidence is linked, the CTA is clear, no unsupported financial claims appear, and there are no real-person likenesses or copyrighted music. List any issues rather than claiming a pass without checking.

## Full voice-over
Diwali outfit reels are everywhere. A new look can feel tempting. But first ask: does it fit your budget? Set a spending limit before you scroll. Protect essentials and savings first. Check your wardrobe. Can you restyle something you already own? If you shop, compare the full price, delivery and return costs. Don't rush. Considering EMI? Check fees, total payable and due dates. Small instalments are still commitments. Choose what you love and can comfortably afford. A discount isn't a saving if you overspend. Save this before festive shopping. Enjoy the celebration, not the bill.

## Safety note
This is general budgeting education, not financial advice. It does not recommend a lender, card, investment or insurance product. Actual EMI terms and fees vary; viewers should read the seller/lender's terms.

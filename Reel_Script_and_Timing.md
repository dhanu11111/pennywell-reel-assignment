# Pennywell – "Complete it?" Reel Script

**Concept:** Complete it? – Half-heart visual
**Duration:** 40 seconds
**Format:** Instagram Reel, 9:16 vertical
**Audience:** Young working adults aged 22–35
**CTA:** Start building your emergency fund today.

| Time | Voice-over | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | What would you do if an unexpected expense came tomorrow? | COMPLETE IT? |
| 5–10s | An emergency can disturb even a carefully planned budget. | UNEXPECTED EXPENSES |
| 10–15s | That's why having an emergency fund matters. | BUILD YOUR SAFETY NET |
| 15–20s | You don't need to save a huge amount at once. | START SMALL |
| 20–25s | Begin with one week of essential expenses. | ONE WEEK OF ESSENTIAL EXPENSES |
| 25–30s | Add a little whenever you can. | SAVE STEP BY STEP |
| 30–35s | Slowly, your financial safety net becomes stronger. | EVERY SMALL STEP COUNTS |
| 35–40s | Complete it? Start building your emergency fund today. | PENNYWELL – START TODAY |

## Visual direction

- Start with a half-completed heart.
- Gradually complete the heart during the Reel.
- Use clean transitions and readable captions.
- Keep the Pennywell branding consistent.
- Use a simple, modern background.

## Final message

An emergency fund helps you prepare for unexpected expenses. Start small and build it gradually.
